Roya Designs

Capstone Project

Designed a CRM-based client portal for Roya Designs to streamline manual client communication and improve operational efficiency. Conducted systems analysis to identify workflow inefficiencies and proposed a centralized information system solution. Designed the system's database schema and developed use case and sequence diagrams to model business processes and system interactions. Produced a detailed implementation plan outlining system architecture, technical specifications, and risk mitigation strategies, and created a high-fidelity UI prototype in Figma to visualize the end-user experience.
